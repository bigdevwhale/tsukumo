## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-23

The first release.

- A pixel-art Japanese house that lives by the real seasons and hours: the tokonoma with a hundred candles, shelves, the kotatsu, the tansu, a paper lamp, and a garden with a mountain, a stone lantern, a pond and a tree that grows with every candle.
- 39 ready-made hobbies in nine families, plus any hobby of your own. Any thing gets one of 21 silhouettes, a colour and a nature, by its name or by chance.
- A session lights a candle: minutes, what you did (six verbs), a note, an optional timer.
- Things wake in five steps and then grow for years: Keeper, Elder, Sage, Kami. Hobby titles by hours, up to 3000.
- Twelve wonders of the house from the 100th to the 2000th candle, four of them rare spirits.
- Friendship between spirits of hobbies practised on the same day, and their little ones.
- No guilt: long breaks put the spirits to sleep under the kotatsu; paused hobbies wait in the storeroom chest.
- The chronicle, year by year. English and Russian. No internet, no ads, no account.
